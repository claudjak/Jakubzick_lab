Join the lab

Prof. Jakubzick is recruiting postdoctoral researchers and PhD students. Contact her if you are interested.

Postdoctoral researchers

We welcome applications from immunologists and cell biologists who want to work on mononuclear phagocytes—macrophage, monocyte and dendritic cell subsets in mouse and human tissues—or on natural antibodies and B cells in cancer immune surveillance. Experience with flow cytometry, single-cell or spatial transcriptomics, mouse models or human tissue is valuable but not required. Send a CV, a short statement of your research interests, and the names of two or three references.

PhD students

Graduate students join the lab through Dartmouth's Molecular and Cellular Biology (MCB) program and the Immunology track at the Geisel School of Medicine. Prospective students are encouraged to get in touch before or during the application process; current Dartmouth students interested in a rotation should email Prof. Jakubzick.

Other visitors

Undergraduates, medical students and visiting scientists interested in the lab's work are also welcome to write.

Our commitment

The Jakubzick Lab and its members are committed to providing an open, inclusive, respectful and collaborative learning and working environment for all. We believe that every individual, regardless of race, age, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, nationality, socio-economic status, disability status or diversity of experience, can find a place where their potential is fulfilled in the Jakubzick Lab community. We are dedicated to celebrating and embracing the differences among us so that we may work toward reducing biases in the scientific community. With greater and more diverse representation, we are confident that our work and the work of our colleagues may benefit from viewpoints that have historically been underrepresented in science.
